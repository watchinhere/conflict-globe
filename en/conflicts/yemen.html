Yemen War / Houthi–Saudi front

Saudi Arabia and allied Yemeni forces launched a major ground and air offensive to retake the Red Sea coast and Bab el-Mandeb area seized by the Houthis a month earlier. On 6–8 October the Houthis struck Riyadh and Abha airports and the Aramco Rabigh refinery with missiles and drones.
