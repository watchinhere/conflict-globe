War

Jihadist insurgency in Burkina Faso

JNIM and Islamic State Sahel Province threaten large parts of the country and fight the military junta. Massacres of civilians and food shortages in blockaded towns are reported.

Region
Northern and eastern Burkina Faso
Began
2015
Status
Escalating

Key developments

  • JNIM has stepped up offensives in Burkina Faso as well as Mali
  • The junta has mobilised volunteer militias (VDP) on a large scale
