Sudanese Civil War (SAF vs RSF)

A civil war between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF). After the RSF took El Fasher in October 2025, the three Kordofan states became the main battlefield. The RSF counterattacked on several fronts in late August while the army retook key points on the Omdurman–El Obeid export road.
