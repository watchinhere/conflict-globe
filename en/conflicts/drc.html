War

Eastern DR Congo / M23 conflict

The Rwanda-backed M23 (AFC) coalition is fighting the Congolese army, Burundian troops and Wazalendo militias. Government forces advanced in southern Masisi in mid-September but stalled toward Ngungu around the 25th. The Minembwe highlands in South Kivu are the fiercest front.
