PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23. Conflict Watch Globe (the “site”) has no accounts or sign-in and does not directly collect personal information such as names or email addresses.

1. Information stored on your device

The site stores your language choice, list sort order and a cache of machine translations in your browser’s local storage. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing site data in your browser removes it.

2. Requests to third-party services

To display maps and live information, your browser contacts the following services directly. They may receive standard access data such as your IP address and browser details, handled under their own policies.

  • Esri (imagery and label tiles), NASA GIBS (night imagery), Mapzen Terrarium on Amazon S3 (elevation), unpkg and jsDelivr (libraries and textures), Google Fonts
  • Wikipedia API (conflict list, current events), the GDELT Project (live articles)
  • Google Translate (only the text being translated, when English-only items are shown in Korean)

3. Advertising and cookies

The site may show Google AdSense ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites. Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before advertising cookies are used.

4. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact page.
